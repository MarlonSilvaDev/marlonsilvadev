### Driven by continuous learning, curiosity, and problem-solving.
My journey in technology began in technical support and the implementation of time tracking and ERP systems, building a strong business vision and hands-on experience in transforming real problems into practical solutions. Today, I am a Full Stack Developer with 3 years of experience in Software Engineering, working on Web, Mobile, and Cross-Platform applications, from requirements gathering and architecture definition to deployment and evolution of digital products in production.

My experience includes technologies such as
Flutter, React Native, React, Next.js, Angular, Node.js, Java (Spring Boot), Kotlin, and TypeScript, as well as REST and SOAP APIs, Microservices, Micro Frontends, BFF, Clean Architecture, SOLID, Design Patterns, MVVM, MVC, JWT, OAuth2, relational databases, Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD. I also participate in technical refinements, requirements gathering, solution definition, architecture decisions, and communication with clients and multidisciplinary teams.

Currently, I am deepening my knowledge in
Cloud Computing (AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud), Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, LLMs, AI-Assisted Development, Python, and Data Science, seeking to apply these technologies to create scalable, secure, efficient solutions with real impact for users and businesses.
